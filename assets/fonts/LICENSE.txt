Typefaces in this folder
========================

DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf - DejaVu Fonts (free to distribute).
See https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/ for the licence.

The other typefaces come from Google Fonts (github.com/google/fonts)
and are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL), unless stated
otherwise. They may be used and bundled freely. Source per family:
https://fonts.google.com/ (search for the name).

Shipped (or fetched once by install.bat):
  Anton, Archivo Black, Audiowide, Barlow Condensed, Bebas Neue, Bungee,
  Concert One, Kanit, Lilita One, Luckiest Guy, Passion One, Righteous,
  Russo One, Saira Condensed, Titan One
  (and optionally the variable fonts Oswald, Montserrat, Fredoka,
   Baloo 2, Rubik when install.bat fetches them).

install.bat checks this folder during installation and fetches only the
missing typefaces; what is already there is not downloaded again.
